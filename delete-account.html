CartDate Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
App: CartDate, by CartDate LLC

1. How to delete your account

In the app

  1. Open CartDate and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.

When in-app deletion is confirmed, your account is deactivated, your profile is hidden, and you are signed out. Deletion of associated data may require additional processing as described below. If your account is suspended, the Delete Account option is available on the suspension screen.

Without the app (email request)

Email hello@cartdate.app with the subject “Delete my CartDate account” and include the email address associated with your account. We will verify that you control the account, generally through the registered email address, before processing your request. Do not send your password, government ID, or payment-card details. We will acknowledge and complete verified requests as promptly as reasonably possible, subject to technical processing and legally required retention.

Deleting the CartDate app from your phone does not delete your account.

2. What happens when you delete your account

When deletion is initiated:

  • Your profile is hidden and removed from Discover.
  • Your matches end. Open date requests and proposals, and upcoming scheduled dates, are cancelled. Date Credit returns for other participants follow the Date Credit Purchase, Cancellation & Refund Policy. Any credits associated with your own cancellation or withdrawal follow that policy as well.
  • Your account access and active sessions are revoked as part of the deletion process.
  • Any unused Date Credits become unavailable upon account deletion. CartDate does not issue an automatic cash refund. Any rights under applicable law or Apple or Google's refund policies remain unaffected.

Data removed as part of account deletion:

  • Your CartDate authentication account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile details, including first name, gender, bio, date of birth, ZIP code, derived location, and dating preferences.
  • Your uploaded profile photo and intro video.
  • Your likes, passes, matches, active date requests, proposals, and scheduled dates, subject to the limited safety and transaction records described in Section 3.
  • Your blocks and in-app notifications.
  • Your Date Credit balance and account-specific consent and standing records, except where a limited record must be retained for the purposes described below.
  • Account-linked check-in attempt data, except limited retained safety review information described below.

Some deletion steps may take additional time, including processing by service providers. If a deletion step fails, our system retries it approximately every 15 minutes for up to seven days. If it still cannot be completed, it is flagged for manual attention rather than retried indefinitely. Your account remains deactivated while outstanding steps are addressed. Records retained for safety, financial, dispute, or legal purposes are described below.

3. What we keep after deletion, and why

We retain limited records when needed for security, safety investigations, financial accounting, refunds, disputes, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Where feasible, identifying links are removed or restricted. Purchase-event copies, deletion records, and audit logs may retain a former account identifier, and previously delivered messages or notes may retain a first name.

Some service providers separately retain information under their own applicable policies or legal obligations:

  • Apple and Google: App-store purchase records associated with your store account are managed by the applicable store.
  • RevenueCat: Purchase and transaction records may be retained as needed for processing, refunds, fraud prevention, or compliance.
  • OneSignal and Postmark: Push notification and transactional-email service records may persist for a limited period under provider retention policies.
  • Supabase: Operational logs and backups may remain for a limited period under applicable service configuration and retention policies.

Retention periods depend on the record type, operational need, provider settings, and applicable law. Access to retained information is restricted to authorized purposes. For questions about a particular retained record or to request further deletion where legally available, contact hello@cartdate.app.

4. Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can remove your profile photo, intro video, and bio through Profile → Edit Profile. To request removal of other specific data without closing your account, email hello@cartdate.app. Requests are evaluated against operational, safety, and legal requirements.

5. Banned or suspended accounts

Banned and suspended members may request account deletion. Deleting an account does not reverse an enforcement decision or automatically entitle a member to restoration of unused Date Credits. Any applicable legal or app-store refund rights remain unaffected.
