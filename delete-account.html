CartDate Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy

Effective date: To be set upon publication App: CartDate, by CartDate LLC

1. How to delete your account

In the app

  1. Open CartDate and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. If your account is suspended, the same Delete Account button is on the suspension screen.

Without the app (web request)

If you no longer have the app, you can request deletion from this page:

Email hello@cartdate.app with the subject “Delete my CartDate account.” Include the email address associated with your account. We will verify that you control the account, typically by responding to the registered email address, before processing the request. Do not send your password, government ID or payment-card details. We will acknowledge the request and complete verified deletion as promptly as reasonably possible, subject to technical processing and legal retention requirements. If we need more information to verify account ownership, we will contact you.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

2. What happens when you delete your account

Immediately:

  • Your profile is hidden and you are removed from Discover.
  • All your matches end. Open date requests and proposals are cancelled and upcoming scheduled dates are cancelled. Date Credits are handled as if you had cancelled or withdrawn (see the Date Credit Purchase, Cancellation & Refund Policy): the other person's credit is returned; yours is returned only for a scheduled date that is 24 hours or more away.
  • You are signed out on all devices.
  • Any unused Date Credits are permanently lost. They are not refunded.

Deleted:

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password).
  • Your profile: first name, gender, bio.
  • Your profile photo and intro video files.
  • Your date of birth, ZIP code and derived city, state and approximate location.
  • Your dating preferences.
  • Your likes, passes, matches, date requests, proposals and scheduled dates, including the copies in the other members' date history.
  • Your blocks and the blocks others placed on you.
  • Your in-app notifications.
  • Your Date Credit balance.
  • Your consent records.
  • Your account-standing record (strikes, suspension, ban).
  • Failed GPS check-in attempts.
  • Your customer record at our purchase processor (RevenueCat).

If any step cannot be completed immediately (for example a temporary outage), your account is already deactivated and hidden, and our system automatically retries until deletion is complete.

3. What we keep after deletion, and why

The following limited records are kept after your account is deleted. Except where stated, they are no longer linked to your account or identity.

Record What is kept Why
Deletion record Your former account ID and the times each deletion step completed To prove the deletion was carried out and to finish any step that needs retrying
Date Credit ledger and purchase records Credit entries and store transaction records (product, transaction identifiers, time, status), with the link to your account removed Financial record-keeping, fraud prevention and handling store refunds or disputes
Reports and no-show reports The report text, any response, the decision and its date, with the links to the people involved removed Safety, and to keep a record of how reports were handled
Verified GPS check-in results Time, distance from the store and accuracy for a date, with the link to you removed. No coordinates are ever stored. Evidence for no-show reviews involving the other participant
Administrative audit log Actions administrators took on your account, their reasons, and your former account ID Accountability for enforcement decisions
Grocery-store requests The store name, ZIP and note you submitted, with the link to you removed To improve the store list

Our service providers may keep their own records as required by their terms or the law:

  • Apple / Google keep your purchase history under your Apple ID or Google account. CartDate cannot delete it.
  • OneSignal (push notifications) and SendGrid (account emails) may hold delivery records. Signing out of CartDate does not by itself guarantee immediate erasure of the OneSignal provider record. You may request provider-data erasure through hello@cartdate.app, subject to identity verification and the provider’s retention policies.
  • Supabase (hosting) keeps server logs and backups for a limited period. Backup and log retention are subject to the provider’s configured policies and applicable law.

Not all retained records have a fixed deletion period implemented. They are retained only as needed for safety, accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal obligations, with access restricted according to purpose. Contact hello@cartdate.app for questions about a retained record.

4. Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can remove your profile photo, intro video and bio at any time in Profile → Edit Profile. To ask for other specific data to be removed without closing your account, email hello@cartdate.app. We will evaluate partial-data requests in light of operational, safety, and legal requirements.

5. Banned or suspended accounts

A banned or suspended member can delete their account in the same way. At present, deletion does not establish a separate permanent ban-prevention identifier. If that practice changes, we will update this notice.
