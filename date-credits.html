CartDate Date Credit Purchase, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: To be set upon publication

1. What a Date Credit is

A Date Credit is an in-app credit used to arrange a CartDate. Date Credits:

  • can only be used inside CartDate;
  • have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money;
  • cannot be transferred to another person or account;
  • do not expire while your account exists.

2. Price and purchase

  • One Date Credit costs $6.99 USD in the United States. The price shown in the app at the time of purchase, including any local currency conversion and taxes applied by the store, is the price you pay.
  • Date Credits are sold one at a time as an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store on iOS. Google Play purchases will be supported only after the Android payment integration is launched.
  • Payment is taken by the relevant app store, not by CartDate. CartDate never receives your card or payment-account details.
  • After the store confirms your purchase, our purchase processor (RevenueCat) notifies our server, which verifies the purchase and adds the credit to your balance. This usually takes a few seconds. A credit is added only after this verification; the app cannot add credits by itself.
  • If a purchase completes but the credit has not appeared, do not buy again. Wait a moment; if it still does not appear, contact hello@cartdate.app.

3. How credits are used: each person uses their own

Arranging a CartDate uses one Date Credit from each participant — two in total. Nobody pays for the other person.

  1. Request (free). After two members match, either one can send a date request. This costs nothing.
  2. Proposal (1 credit — the person who received the request). The other member chooses a grocery store and three possible times and sends them. One Date Credit is taken from that member when the proposal is sent.
  3. Unlock (1 credit — the person who made the request). The requester uses one Date Credit to see the store and times.
  4. Response. The requester then accepts one of the times, sends one counter-proposal with a different store or times, or declines. If a counter-proposal is sent, the other member accepts one of the new times or declines.
  5. Scheduled. When a time is accepted the date is scheduled.

4. When credits are returned to your CartDate balance

These are returns of in-app Date Credits to your CartDate balance. They are not refunds of money.

A date cannot be cancelled in the app after its start time.

Rescheduling

  • Rescheduling is free and does not use or return credits.
  • A reschedule can be requested only while the date is more than 2 hours away.
  • Each scheduled date can have one reschedule decision: once a reschedule request has been accepted or declined, that date cannot be rescheduled again. A request you withdraw, or one that expires, does not count.
  • The original date stays in place unless the other person accepts a new time.

If a participant is suspended or banned, CartDate tells the other participant that the date is under review and decides at least 12 hours before the start time. If no decision has been made by then, the date is cancelled automatically under the rules above.

5. Refunds of money

  • CartDate does not issue cash refunds and cannot refund a purchase itself. All purchases are made through Apple or Google and are subject to their refund policies.
  • To ask for a refund of an iOS purchase, use Apple’s refund request service at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. For any future Google Play purchase, use Google Play’s refund request process.
  • If Apple or Google refunds or reverses a purchase, the Date Credit from that purchase is removed from your CartDate balance if it has not been used.
  • Except where the law requires otherwise, purchases of Date Credits are final. This does not limit mandatory refund or cancellation rights under applicable law.

6. Unused credits

  • Unused Date Credits stay in your balance and do not expire.
  • A permanent ban ends access to CartDate, including the ability to use any remaining Date Credits. A temporary suspension pauses access to credits until the suspension ends. Any refund rights under applicable law or the app store remain unaffected.
  • If you delete your account, any unused Date Credits in that account become unavailable and are removed with the account. No automatic cash refund is issued by CartDate. Any refund rights provided by applicable law or the app store remain unaffected.

7. Errors

If you believe a credit was taken or not returned in error, email hello@cartdate.app with the date and the other member's first name. We review the ledger for your account and correct genuine errors. Corrections are reviewed by authorized support personnel; do not share your payment-card information.
