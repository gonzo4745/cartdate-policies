Child Safety Standards

Policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Effective date: October 14, 2026

CartDate is an adults-only dating service. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and maintaining a platform that does not enable, promote, or tolerate harm to minors.

1. Adults only

CartDate is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 or older. People under 18 may not register for or use CartDate. Accounts found to belong to minors may be disabled or removed. Providing a false age or impersonating another person to evade this restriction is prohibited.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

CartDate expressly prohibits all conduct and content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, including:

  • Creating, possessing through the service, uploading, sharing, distributing, requesting, soliciting, or facilitating child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including real, altered, or computer-generated depictions prohibited by applicable law.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to establish sexual contact with a minor.
  • Sexual exploitation, coercion, sextortion, trafficking, or the commercial sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Using profiles, images, videos, descriptions, date invitations, or other service features to sexualize minors or facilitate their abuse or exploitation.
  • Encouraging, arranging, promoting, or linking to child sexual exploitation or related illegal activity.

These prohibitions apply to every account and all content and conduct involving CartDate, whether or not a particular activity takes place through an in-app messaging feature. CartDate does not offer in-app chat.

3. Reporting child safety concerns

Users can report suspicious profiles, harmful content, or inappropriate behavior using CartDate's in-app reporting and safety/support features. To report suspected CSAE or CSAM directly, email hello@cartdate.app with a description and relevant account or incident details.

Do not send, forward, download, or attach suspected CSAM. Instead, provide enough non-graphic information to identify the concern. Reports will be reviewed and addressed as appropriate.

Immediate danger: Contact local emergency services or law enforcement. In the United States, suspected child sexual exploitation can be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.

4. Enforcement and response

When CartDate becomes aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we will assess the report promptly, take appropriate action to restrict access to prohibited material and accounts, and act in accordance with applicable law. Actions may include disabling or permanently banning accounts, removing content, and preserving relevant information where required or permitted by law.

CartDate will report apparent or confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or other appropriate authorities when legally required and will cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement. We do not permit users to evade enforcement by creating replacement accounts.

5. Privacy and responsible handling

Child-safety reports and associated information are handled with appropriate confidentiality and access restrictions, consistent with our Privacy Policy, applicable law, and the need to investigate, prevent, and report abuse. We do not promise absolute confidentiality where disclosure is required to protect children or comply with law.

6. Designated child safety contact

Child safety concerns, including notifications from Google Play and requests from appropriate authorities, can be directed to CartDate at hello@cartdate.app.

CartDate is responsible for reviewing these notices and taking appropriate action under this policy.

7. Policy updates

We may update these standards to reflect changes in our services, applicable laws, or child-safety practices. The current version will remain publicly available on this page.
