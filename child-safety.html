Child Safety Standards

Policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Effective date: October 14, 2026

CartDate is an adults-only dating service. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit content and conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children, and we review reports and take action under the standards below.

1. Adults only

CartDate is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 or older. People under 18 may not register for or use CartDate. Accounts identified as belonging to minors are subject to removal or disabling. Providing a false age or impersonating another person to evade this restriction is prohibited.

2. Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

CartDate expressly prohibits all conduct and content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation, including:

  • Creating, uploading, storing, sharing, distributing, requesting, soliciting, or facilitating child sexual abuse material (CSAM) through CartDate, including real, altered, or computer-generated depictions prohibited by applicable law.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to arrange sexual contact with a minor.
  • Sexual exploitation, coercion, sextortion, trafficking, or the commercial sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Using profiles, photographs, videos, biographies, date invitations, or other CartDate features to sexualize minors or facilitate their abuse or exploitation.
  • Encouraging, arranging, promoting, or directing others to child sexual exploitation or related illegal activity.

These prohibitions apply to accounts, content, and activity involving use of CartDate. CartDate does not offer in-app chat.

3. In-app reporting, blocking, and direct contact

CartDate provides Report and Block functions within the app. Users can report suspicious profiles, inappropriate conduct, or child safety concerns using the in-app Report feature. Submitted reports generate emails for CartDate to review and follow up on. Users may also block another user through the app to prevent unwanted interaction. Blocking a user is not a substitute for submitting a report when abuse or exploitation is suspected.

Child safety concerns can also be reported directly to our monitored contact address, hello@cartdate.app. Include a description of the concern, the relevant profile or account details, and any non-graphic information that may help us review it.

Do not send, forward, download, or attach suspected CSAM. Report the concern without redistributing potentially illegal material.

CartDate reviews incoming reports and safety emails. Reports suggesting child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as priority safety concerns and evaluated for appropriate action. Users who submit reports may be contacted for additional information when needed.

Immediate danger: Contact local emergency services or law enforcement. In the United States, suspected child sexual exploitation can also be reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.

4. Review, enforcement, and removal

When CartDate receives a child-safety report or otherwise becomes aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we review the available information and take appropriate action in accordance with this policy and applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, action may include investigating an account or profile, restricting access, removing prohibited content, disabling an account, or permanently banning an offending user.

When CartDate obtains actual knowledge of CSAM on its service, we take appropriate steps to remove or disable access to the material, handle relevant information lawfully, and make reports to appropriate authorities as required by applicable law. CartDate prohibits attempts to evade enforcement through replacement accounts.

We do not tolerate retaliation against people who report child-safety concerns in good faith.

5. Compliance with child safety laws and reporting to authorities

CartDate complies with applicable child safety laws and reporting obligations. Reports indicating possible CSAM or child exploitation are evaluated to determine whether they require escalation or reporting. We maintain a process for reporting confirmed or otherwise reportable CSAM to NCMEC or the appropriate regional or national authority, as required by applicable law.

We cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement and take appropriate steps to preserve relevant information where legally required or permitted. We do not require users to investigate suspected abuse themselves or to transmit CSAM to CartDate.

6. Privacy and responsible handling

Child-safety reports and associated information are handled with appropriate confidentiality and access restrictions, consistent with our Privacy Policy, applicable law, and the need to investigate, prevent, and report abuse. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality when disclosure is necessary to protect children or comply with law.

7. Designated child safety contact

CartDate monitors hello@cartdate.app for child safety reports and related notices. This address is our designated contact for users, Google Play, NCMEC, and appropriate authorities regarding child sexual abuse material prevention, incident review, enforcement procedures, and compliance.

CartDate is responsible for reviewing notices received at this address, following up as needed, and taking appropriate action under this policy.

8. Policy updates

We may update these standards to reflect changes in our services, applicable laws, or child-safety practices. The current version will remain publicly available on this page.
