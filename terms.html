9. No-shows

  • If the other person does not attend a scheduled date, you can report a no-show in the app from the date's start time until 48 hours afterwards.
  • A report is only a report. It is not a strike and has no effect on the reported member's account by itself.
  • The reported member is notified and can submit one written response in the app before the report is reviewed.
  • An administrator reviews the report, any response and any check-in records, and either confirms or dismisses it.
  • Only a confirmed no-show counts as a strike. After one confirmed no-show the member receives a warning.
  • When an administrator confirms a member's second no-show, the system automatically and permanently bans that account, ends their matches and cancels their open requests and upcoming dates under the normal credit rules. This automatic step happens only as a result of the administrator's second confirmation.
  • A confirmed no-show can be appealed and overturned, which removes the strike.
  • A no-show does not result in a Date Credit being returned to either participant, including the member who attended. This avoids creating incentives for false or coordinated no-show claims. Only an administrator-confirmed no-show creates a strike.
