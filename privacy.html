CartDate Privacy Policy

Effective date: To be set upon publication Policy version: 2026-10 (draft)

CartDate ("CartDate", "we", "us") is operated by CartDate LLC. This policy explains what information the CartDate mobile app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

CartDate currently supports U.S. ZIP-code-based discovery and grocery-store locations. Service availability may vary by location.

1. Adults only

CartDate is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. We ask for a date of birth at sign-up and refuse accounts whose date of birth shows the person is under 18. We do not verify age with government ID, selfies or photo analysis. If you believe someone under 18 is using CartDate, report the profile in the app (choose "Appears to be under 18") or email hello@cartdate.app. We review reports and may suspend or remove accounts found or reasonably believed to belong to minors.

Information created when you use CartDate

  • Activity: likes, passes, matches, blocks, date requests, proposals, scheduled dates, reschedules, cancellations and their times.
  • Date Credits: your balance and a ledger of credits bought, used and returned.
  • Purchases: the store product, transaction identifiers, purchase time and status we receive from Apple or Google through RevenueCat. We never receive your card number or payment account details.
  • Consent records: that you accepted the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy and confirmed you are 18+, with the document version and time.
  • Notifications: the in-app notices we generate for you.
  • Account standing: confirmed no-show strikes, suspensions and bans.
  • Device and push information: if you allow notifications, a push token and device information held by our push provider and linked to your account ID.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address, time and request type). The provider’s log-retention period depends on its service configuration.

Location

  • ZIP-based location. From your ZIP code we look up the city, state and the approximate centre point of that ZIP code. We use this to show you members and grocery stores near you. We do not use your phone's GPS for discovery.
  • GPS Check-In (optional, limited). If you tap Check In for a scheduled date, the app asks for your phone's location at that moment only. The reading (latitude, longitude, accuracy and time) is sent to our server, which checks whether you are near the store. We do not store the coordinates. We store only the result: whether the check-in was verified, the time, the distance from the store and the reported accuracy. Failed attempts are logged without coordinates and deleted after 30 days. CartDate never tracks your location in the background and never requests location when the app starts.

What we do not collect

We do not collect contacts, calendars, health data, advertising identifiers, or browsing history, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics software. This statement reflects the reviewed version of the app.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and confirm you are an adult.
  • To show you compatible members based on mutual gender preference, age range and distance.
  • To let matched members request, propose, schedule, reschedule and cancel grocery-store dates.
  • To sell Date Credits and keep an accurate credit balance.
  • To send in-app and push notifications about your matches, dates and account.
  • To verify arrival at a date when you choose to check in.
  • To review reports, investigate misconduct, confirm or dismiss no-show reports, and suspend or ban accounts.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with law.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

4. What other members can see

Members who can view your profile see your first name, age, gender, bio, profile photo, intro video, city and state, and approximate distance (in miles, based on ZIP codes).

Other members never see your email address, date of birth, ZIP code, exact location, credit balance or purchase history.

A member you are matched with also sees the dates, times and stores you propose or agree to, any notes you write to them, and, once you have checked in for a date, that you have arrived. They never see your distance, accuracy or coordinates.

If another member files a no-show report about you, you are told that a report was filed, but not the reporter's written details.

Photos and videos are stored in private storage and shown through temporary links to signed-in members.

5. What CartDate administrators can see

Authorised CartDate administrators, who must sign in with a second authentication factor, can view: your first name, email address, age, gender, bio, city and state, account status, confirmed no-show strikes, reports about you and by you, no-show reports and responses, date check-in results (time, distance, accuracy — not coordinates), and the history of administrative actions on your account. Staff with direct database access can also view the other information described in this policy, including photos and videos, where needed to investigate reports or operate the service. Administrative decisions are recorded in an audit log.

7. Retention

We keep your information while your account exists. We have not set fixed retention periods for most records; the specific rules that exist today are:

  • Failed GPS check-in attempts: deleted after 30 days.
  • Deleted accounts: see the Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy for what is deleted immediately and what limited records are kept.

We retain certain records for safety, financial accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and legal compliance as described in the Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy. We do not currently specify a fixed deletion schedule for each retained record.

8. Your choices

  • Edit your profile in the app at any time. Photo, video and bio can be removed.
  • Push notifications can be turned off in your phone's settings.
  • Location for Check-In can be denied or turned off in your phone's settings; you can still use CartDate but cannot check in.
  • Block a member through currently available profile controls; if you cannot access a member's Block control, email hello@cartdate.app to report a safety concern. Matched-member reporting and blocking controls are being expanded.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or request deletion without the app by emailing hello@cartdate.app as described in the Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy.
  • Access or correction requests: email hello@cartdate.app. We may ask you to verify account ownership before providing account-specific data. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights.

9. Security

Access to data is restricted at the database level so that members can read only their own private records, and administrative functions require an administrator account with a second authentication factor. Photos and videos are in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee security.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the effective date. When material changes require additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide it through an appropriate channel. The app records policy acceptance at sign-up; it does not currently require renewed acceptance for each policy update.
