CartDate Support

Need help with CartDate? We're here to assist with account access, Date Credits, scheduling, reporting, and other app questions.

Contact us

Email hello@cartdate.app for support.

To help us look into your request, include the email address associated with your CartDate account, a brief description of the issue, and any relevant error message. Do not send your password, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Common support topics

  • Account and sign-in: Email confirmation, password resets, and profile questions.
  • Date Credits: Missing purchases, credit balances, cancellations, and credit returns. If a purchase completed but your credit is missing, contact us before purchasing again.
  • Dates: Requests, proposals, scheduling, rescheduling, cancellations, and check-in questions.
  • Safety: You can report concerns within CartDate or contact us by email. For immediate danger, contact emergency services.
  • Account deletion: Delete your account in CartDate under Profile → Delete Account, or see our Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy for an email request option.

Policies and information

Read our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Date Credit Purchase, Cancellation & Refund Policy, and Account Deletion & Data Removal Policy.
